**H60a: bounded short reply continuity.** The previous seven-case cohort suggested
unnecessary short-factual refresh latency. Fresh normal gateway reproduction confirms
one short factual answer made two accounted provider attempts and posted unchanged
wording. The adapter's 24-character full-echo threshold treats four words as
substantial repetition. Seventeen new same-case acceptance checks fail on baseline;
two substantial-echo controls pass. The causal hypothesis is that exempting drafts
with at most five lexical words and sixty characters avoids cosmetic rechecks and
regeneration, so that brief stable answers remain direct and responsive.

This recognizes size, not truth, intent, naturalness or an Andrew habit. It preserves
initial source verification, attribution and withdrawal; only the optional repetition
path changes. Longer repeated jokes, twelve-word passages and independent brevity
repair remain eligible. A short joke may repeat without automatic novelty recovery.
That deliberate boundary must be reported, rather than claiming semantic repetition
is solved. Long single tokens remain substantial through the character ceiling.

Acceptance locked before candidate provider results: affected same-case drafts use
one accounted attempt, no cosmetic freshness callback, and retain exact supported
content or uncertainty. Six-word and over-sixty-character controls retain recovery.
Three-sentence short drafts still get brevity repair and its source check. Existing
failure, cancellation, purpose, budget, quote, image, author and recipient controls
must pass. Use identical frozen first drafts in both adapter arms to separate harness
effects from model sampling. Then review a bounded same-model synthetic handler cohort
and actual candidate owner/handler send. After release, actual normal gateway baseball,
historical recall, exact-date uncertainty, care, humor and recipient controls must pass.
Measure attempts and latency separately; no aggregate latency claim from sequential
or stochastic arms. Disconfirming evidence includes stale private reuse, invented facts,
lost longer-echo recovery, insensitive care, extra attempts or a changed first request.

**H61: selective context tools.** Potential benefit: gather relevant current context
only when useful, so factual answers can be grounded without loading unrelated material.

describes custom functions and deferred tool loading. Those are documented capabilities,
not tested Trubot quality, model compatibility, latency or budget results. A bounded
local retrieval tool with lifecycle checks and no authority from channel text is a
candidate architecture experiment. Acceptance requires fewer irrelevant packets,
matched groundedness/abstention, controlled tool failures and a USD 20 operating estimate.
It remains exploratory. Generated Michigan U.P. aurora and trail-camera scenes also
remain exploratory, requiring source-pixel study, sharing/timing evidence, short captions,
generated provenance and operating-cost validation. No Astra or paid service selected.
