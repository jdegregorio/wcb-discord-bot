# Corrective evaluation: 2026-10-05

Version 2.1.1 passed its 28 candidate samples but failed one of 14 production
rubric checks: an uncertain playoff answer added an unsupported negative fandom
claim. Its actual outputs and health are retained. The release was withdrawn
from stable discovery without changing its tag or digest, and production was
restored to the recorded healthy 2.1.0 image.

## Correction and evidence

Baseball allegiance is explicitly unspecified. Current sports facts without
evidence require uncertainty or a request for an update. A new direct
allegiance probe guards against inventing a permanent preference. Inferred
follow-ups use low reasoning with a 512-token total output ceiling. Direct,
reaction, and ambient replies retain no reasoning and a 180-token ceiling.
The same low-cost GPT-6 Luna model uses standard processing, low verbosity,
storage disabled, and cache key v6. Existing configured token ceilings still
apply. No second generation pass is introduced.

The final candidate ran 30 synthetic samples through the real Discord event
handlers and Responses adapter, with history and posting captured by fixtures.
No Discord login or writes occurred. All 30 generations and transport checks
passed with complete usage and no provider errors. Agent review read every
output against the case rubric and accepted 30/30. This is a small, unblinded
review by the implementing agent, not a human fidelity study.
