## Phase 1: evidence and hypotheses

Authenticated current audit: three intake channels, latest 100 each, fourteen days,
seven target, twelve peer and four older bot turns. Five attributed target turns,
three older bot turns, peer setup and an explicit reply were inspected in the UI.
Zero current spontaneous replies; older pessimism and mechanical acknowledgement
predate current production. One image-dependent turn is excluded from interpretation
because its pixels were not inspected. This is a contextual audit, not a new trait
or an independent human-held-out benchmark. No human care-style claim is established.

A fresh owner-authored private development question reproduces the care defect:
a relevant, sincere answer has three short sentences and a stock final offer.
The previous run showed the same category. Existing product guidance already asks
for at most two brief sentences; the issue is execution of that contract, rather
than a newly inferred habit. Development probes never enter Andrew learning.

H59: bounded brief-reply repair. Mechanism: recognize plainly overlong ordinary
conversational drafts and share the existing single optional quality attempt with
stale-reply recovery. Request a fresh one- or two-sentence answer, preserving specific
care, comic premise, supported facts, uncertainty and recipient judgment. So that
Trubot sounds less like a formulaic three-part acknowledgement while retaining warmth.
Baseline: no correction for verbosity unless the wording independently repeats an
older reply. Uncertainty: a length heuristic is not a semantic judge or proof of
Andrew fidelity, and an extra request adds latency/cost. Disconfirm: cold compression,
new facts, broken quotations/detailed answers, insensitive jokes, recipient mistakes,
third quality attempts, private reuse without source checks or spending bypass.

Locked acceptance before candidate provider results: same-model matched synthetic
care/celebration/banter/factual/recipient cases, three samples per arm. All final care
and celebration replies fit two brief sentences and retain their specific emotional
meaning. No control regression in groundedness, relevance, identity or abstention.
Mechanical controls cover quotations, detail/evidence requests, abbreviation/decimal/
quoted punctuation, absent focus, correction failures, cancellation, source freshness,
shared attempt limit, accounting and no truncation. Fresh real Discord care plus
baseball, broad historical recall, banter and recipient controls required after release.
Report attempts, latency and ledger cost separately from semantic review.
