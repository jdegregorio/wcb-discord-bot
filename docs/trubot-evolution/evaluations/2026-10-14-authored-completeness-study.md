# Complete stored authored reply evidence

## Decision and scope

Ship H68 as an enabling source-integrity correction. The renderer formerly sent
only the first 1,500 characters of an attributed stored passage. It now sends the
whole stored passage within 6,000 UTF-8 bytes, or an empty text field and explicit
internal gap. An omitted passage loses its adjacent context too, preventing peer
positions from standing in for absent authored meaning. Source provenance remains.
All-omitted packets disable optional voice examples.

This does not fix H66 historical synthesis or establish a new human trait. Current
private coverage is 405 eligible archive blocks and 34 native messages, with zero
passages past the old character boundary. The separate native intake storage cap
is unchanged. Renderer completeness is not complete original-message coverage.
