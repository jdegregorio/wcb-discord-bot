  events. All source, provider, validation and spending failures share one warning.

  not prove the older events resolved or reveal their causes.
- Mechanism and benefit: fixed cause categories and pipeline stages distinguish
  a safe abstention from unavailable learning, so that operators can repair the
  limiting part of the evidence loop before expanding character memory.
- Baseline: nine distinct injected failure paths yield one warning category;
  five completion outcomes do not explain abstention/rejection. No payload leaks.
- Predetermined acceptance: classify all 14 same-case probes; distinguish budget,
  timeout, source validation, storage and unexpected failures; preserve pacing
  across restart, source authority, rejected-write isolation and cancellation;
  emit no source/answer text, identifiers, paths, exception messages or bodies.
  Normal paced/verification/no-candidate polls must not emit warnings.
- Disconfirming evidence: wrong source-check stage, private payload in a log,
  repeated request after restart, changed learning decisions, false completion
  after a failed commit, or degraded real gateway replies.
- Method: production-package baseline/candidate deterministic fault injection
  using disposable synthetic stores and mocked transports, plus adapter/handler
  regression, installed-wheel probes and real owner-authored Discord checks.
- Results: baseline 0/14 diagnosed, candidate 14/14 diagnosed; privacy and pacing
  preserved in 14/14. Zero provider calls or Discord writes in these probes.
  Final installed, real gateway and health results follow in progress.md.
- Decision: supported enabling increment. It measures diagnosis, not reply-quality
  improvement. Earlier generic warnings cannot be retrospectively classified.
